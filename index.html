About Me

I live in Michoacan, Mexico, and I teach English online every day. Most of my week is spent in one-on-one lessons and small evening groups, helping adults get comfortable speaking a language they were afraid of for years.

I also work as a student mentor, which is where I learned that a clear message beats a clever one. That is the same idea I want to bring into the pages I build.

You can see what I am working on in my GitHub profile, or reach me through LinkedIn.

Why I Am Taking This Course

Teaching taught me how people learn; I want to build the tools they learn with. In WDD 131 I am moving past static pages into JavaScript, so a site can respond to what someone actually does on it.

My goal this semester is simple: write markup that validates, style it so it reads well on a phone, and keep every page fast enough that a student on a slow connection is not left waiting. When I get stuck I usually end up on MDN Web Docs.
