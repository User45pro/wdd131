How a class works

  1. You talk first

    We start with a question about your week. You speak for a few minutes and I listen without stopping you.

  2. I write the mistakes down

    Every sentence that came out wrong goes on my list, with the way a native speaker would say it. We go back over the list together.

  3. You get a summary

    The same night you receive one page: the topic for next time, your mistakes, and the words you were missing.

Pick your level

Your teacher

I am William, I live in Michoacan and I teach English online every day, most of it one to one. I also work as a student mentor, so I spend my week listening to adults who are afraid of saying the wrong thing.

If you can say it in Spanish, you should be able to say it in English. So we start from what you already live and we build the English around it.

What people ask me

I studied English at school and I forgot it. Can I still start?
Yes, and it is the most common case. Most of it is still there, you just have not used it in years. The beginner classes bring it back in order.
What if I get stuck and I cannot say something?
You say it in Spanish and we build the English sentence together. That is where most of the class comes from.
How long is a class and how often?
Between forty five and sixty minutes, twice a week for most courses. The full days and times are on the Courses page.
Can I try before I decide?
That is what the first class is for. It is free, it is a real class, and at the end you know which course fits you.
