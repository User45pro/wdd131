Site Plan for My WDD 131 Final Project

Site Name

Clear Voice English

I chose this name because my students do not need perfect grammar on day one; they need a clear voice they trust. Every class I teach is built around speaking, so the name promises what the site delivers: a conversation school that helps adults in Mexico say what they mean in English.

Domain idea, not purchased yet: clearvoice-english.com

Site Purpose

The site is the page I send to a new student who asks me how my online English classes work. It explains the program and how a class runs, lists the courses I teach with their level, days, time and length, and gives visitors a short form to request a free first class. The site also remembers the level a visitor picked, so it is still selected when that person comes back.

Pages

  • Home: the program, how a class runs and who it is for.
  • Courses: every course, with buttons to filter by beginner, intermediate or advanced.
  • Free Class: a form with validation and a confirmation message.
  • References: sources for images and text, linked in the footer of every page.

Scenarios

These are the questions a visitor from my target audience will ask, and each one decides what the site must answer.

  1. Which class is right for my level, and how do I find out?
  2. On which days and at what time can I join, and how long is each session?
  3. Can I try a class for free before I decide?
  4. Will the teacher understand my situation as a Spanish speaker?

Typography

I use two fonts, both loaded from Google Fonts, with a safe fallback for each.

  • Roboto Slab for the site name, page titles and headings. Say it clearly.
  • Open Sans for paragraphs, forms, buttons and navigation. Your first class is on us.
