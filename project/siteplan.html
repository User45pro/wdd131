Site Plan for My WDD 131 Final Project

Site Name

Clear Voice English

Most of my students can read and understand English. What stops them is speaking, because they are afraid of making mistakes. I wanted a name about having a clear voice and not about grammar rules. It is short and easy to remember.

Domain idea: clearvoice-english.com (I have not bought it).

Site Purpose

This is the page I would send to someone who writes to me and asks how my classes work. It explains what a class looks like and shows the courses I teach, with the level, the days, the time and how long each one lasts. It also has a short form to ask for a free first class. When a visitor picks a level, the site remembers it for the next visit.

The site has three pages: Home, Courses and Free Class. There is also a References page, and it is linked in the footer.

Scenarios

These are the questions I think a visitor will ask when they land on the site.

  1. I studied English in school but I forgot most of it. Which class should I take?
  2. What days and what time are the classes? How long does each one last?
  3. Can I try a class first, before I decide?
  4. What happens if I get stuck and I cannot say something in English?
